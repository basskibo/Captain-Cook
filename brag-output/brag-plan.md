# Captain Cook — brag plan

**What:** A personal AI cook in your pocket: tap what's in your kitchen, get three dishes with step-by-step recipes.
**For:** Anyone standing at the fridge at 7pm asking "Šta da skuvam?"
**Sets it apart:** It's a real, polished mobile PWA. Picking ingredients takes a few taps, it shows a cooking animation while it thinks, and cooking mode turns "kuvaj 8 minuta" into a timer button.
**Funniest claim:** The loading copy: "Zagrevam tiganj…", "Seckam luk (bez suza)…".
**Visual hook:** The app's own button text, "Šta da skuvam?", set huge, with the five ingredients popping in around it.
**Real UI shown:** A screen recording of the real app at 2× in a phone frame: pantry chips → options sheet → frying-pan animation → 3 AI recipe cards (real Gemini output) → recipe → cooking mode → timer.
**Tone:** default: punchy, playful, clean. The app's warm cream background (#f7f3ee) and its orange gradient (#f0562b → #ff9f43), set in Bricolage Grotesque and Geist.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt
